One short list of what actually needs you.

PulseList reads the letters, screenshots and reminders already on your iPhone, puts the few things with a date on them on one list, and stays quiet about everything else.

It reads on your iPhone

Photograph a letter or hand over a screenshot. PulseList works out what it is asking of you, on the device.

It never writes back

Reminders and Calendar are read, never changed. Clearing something here leaves what is in Apple's own apps exactly as it was.

It stays quiet

An event earns a place only when it asks something of you before it happens. Everything else it reads is left alone.
